Once your gross revenue exceeds EUR 25,000, and until your gross revenue exceeds EUR 250,000 (at which point Section 7.1 applies instead), you agree to pay The Publisher a royalty of:

    5% of gross revenue derived from applications using the Software.

- Season passes, battle passes, and other time-limited paid content
- Subscription revenue attributable to access to the application
- In-game advertising revenue
- Merchandising, licensing, and media rights revenue directly attributable to the application

This list is illustrative and not exhaustive. Any revenue stream generated directly through, within, or by virtue of an application built with the Software falls within "gross revenue" regardless of how it is labeled or structured.
